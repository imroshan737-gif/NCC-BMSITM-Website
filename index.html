The Aims of NCC

01

To develop character, comradeship, discipline, leadership, a secular outlook, the spirit of adventure, and ideals of selfless service among the youth of the country.

02

To create a human resource of organized, trained, and motivated youth to provide leadership in all walks of life and be always available for service of the nation.

03

To provide a suitable environment to motivate the youth to take up a career in the Armed Forces.

"Since 2016, NCC BMSITM has represented the institution through training, ceremonial service, national camps, and community-led outreach."

About the Unit

The unit operates as 39/17 NCC Company under 39 Karnataka Battalion, Bangalore B Group, Karnataka and Goa Directorate. The unit is based at BMSITM and trains cadets in parade discipline, map reading, weapon training, and camp progression.

With an authorised strength of 52 cadets, the unit has achieved national representation through RDC, AITSC, Para Basic camps, and other national camps.

Dr. P Dayananda Pai, Chairman, BMSCE

Dr. P. Dayananda Pai, Chairman of BMS College of Engineering (BMSCE), is a distinguished former NCC Cadet with an accomplished record of service and achievement.

During his NCC tenure, he served as a Senior Under Officer (SUO) of the Mysore Contingent and proudly represented Mysore State at the Republic Day Camp and Republic Day Parade (RDC-1965), held at Rajpath, New Delhi, on 26 January 1965. In recognition of this achievement, he received the award for representing Mysore State at RDC-1965 from the then Prime Minister of India, Pandit Jawaharlal Nehru.

Beyond his accomplishments in the NCC, Dr. Pai was also a University Champion in Weightlifting, demonstrating exceptional discipline, physical strength, and competitive spirit.

His journey as an NCC cadet reflects a remarkable combination of leadership, discipline, physical excellence, and dedication to the nation—values that continue to define his distinguished career.
