About NCC BMSITM

Formal training, ceremonial discipline, and service-led leadership since 2016.

NCC Emblem

The National Cadet Corps

The National Cadet Corps is India's premier uniformed youth organization, nurturing discipline, leadership, and service among students across the country. It brings together motivated cadets from schools and colleges into a tri-services environment shaped by the Army, Navy, and Air Force ethos.

At BMSITM, the NCC unit upholds the motto "Unity and Discipline" through structured training, parade practice, and community outreach aligned with national values.

The Three Aims of NCC

01

To develop character, comradeship, discipline, leadership, a secular outlook, the spirit of adventure, and ideals of selfless service among the youth of the country.

02

To create a human resource of organized, trained, and motivated youth to provide leadership in all walks of life and be always available for service of the nation.

03

To provide a suitable environment to motivate the youth to take up a career in the Armed Forces.
